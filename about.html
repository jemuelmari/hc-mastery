About This App

Mastery-based learning units with quizzes (90% passing threshold) covering thermotherapy, cryotherapy, and hot sitz bath.
Interactive mastery games to reinforce indications, contraindications, and physiologic effects.
30-item PNLE-aligned capstone exam with one attempt and a Certificate of Completion.
Offline-first PWA — works with or without internet after first load.
Auto-submits progress to the instructor via Google Sheets backend.
Instructor Dashboard & Gradebook with Philippine transmutation (1.00–5.00).
